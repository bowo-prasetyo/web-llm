# 8. Offline use, installing, and moving your data

## Use the app offline

After you've opened the app once while online, it works **without an internet connection**.

**Works offline:**

* Opening the app
* Chatting with any model you've already downloaded (marked ✓ in the model menu)
* Reading, searching and deleting your saved chats
* Attaching documents (for large documents, once you've attached one large document while online, so the search helper is saved)

**Needs a connection:**

* Downloading a model you don't have yet
* The **first** large document you ever attach (the app downloads a small 23 MB search helper once)

When you're offline, an **Offline** badge appears in the status bar. If you pick a model that isn't downloaded, the app explains that it needs a connection.

To check that the app is ready for offline use, open **Settings → This device → Works offline**. It should say *"Yes, the app is saved on this device."*

> **Tip:** Before a flight or a trip, open the app once while online and make sure your favorite model shows ✓.

## Install the app

You can install Hearth so it opens in its own window, like a regular app, with an icon on your desktop, dock or home screen.

### Chrome or Edge (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android)

1. Open **Settings → This device**.
2. Tap **Install app** and confirm.

If you don't see the button, use your browser's own menu: look for **Install Hearth** or **Add to Home screen**. In Chrome and Edge on a computer there's often an install icon in the address bar.

### Safari on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the app in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

### Safari on Mac

In Safari, choose **File → Add to Dock**.

The installed app uses the same chats, settings and models as the browser version.

## App updates

Updates arrive automatically when you're online; you don't need to do anything.

If you leave a tab open while a new version comes out, a bar says **"A new version of the app is available."**

* Tap **Reload** to update now. If a reply is being written, the button says **Reload after this reply** and waits for it to finish.
* Tap **×** to dismiss it and update later.

Your chats and settings are kept when the app updates.

## Back up and move your data

Your chats live only in **this browser on this device**. They don't sync automatically. Use Export and Import to make a backup or move to another browser, computer or phone.

### Back up your chats

1. Open **Settings → Export / Import**.
2. Under **💬 Chat History**, tap **Export Chats**.
3. A file named like `hearth-chats-2026-11-04.json` is downloaded. Keep it somewhere safe.

The file includes all your chats, messages and attached documents.

> **Important:** The export file contains everything you've written in the app, in readable form. Store and share it with the same care you would a private diary.

### Back up your settings

Under **⚙ Settings**, tap **Export Settings**. A file named like `hearth-settings-2026-11-04.json` is downloaded.

### Move to another device or browser

1. On the **old** device, export your chats (and settings, if you like) as described above.
2. Copy the file(s) to the **new** device, for example by email to yourself, a USB stick, or cloud storage.
3. On the new device, open the app and go to **Settings → Export / Import**.
4. Tap **Import Chats** and choose the chats file. Tap **Import Settings** and choose the settings file.

**About importing:**

* Imported chats are **added** to the chats already on the device. Nothing is overwritten.
* Chats that are already there are recognized and skipped, so importing the same file twice won't create duplicates.
* Importing settings keeps the model that's already selected on the new device, so it never triggers an unexpected download. Choose a model afterwards as usual.

> **Note:** Models are **not** included in exports (they're several gigabytes). The new device downloads its own model the first time.

## Where your data is stored

Everything stays in your browser's storage on this device:

* Chats and attached documents
* Settings and personas
* Downloaded AI models
* A copy of the app itself, for offline use

**Clearing this site's data** in your browser settings removes all of it. Export first if you want to keep your chats.
