# Hearth 1.0.0

**A private AI chat that lives in your browser.** No server, no account, no data leaves your device, and it works offline once a model is downloaded.

**Try it: https://bowo-prasetyo.github.io/web-llm/**

## Highlights

- **Private by design.** The AI model runs on your own GPU. Your messages, chats and documents stay in your browser.
- **No setup.** Answer two questions (what it's for, and a size) and Hearth picks a model that suits your device. Downloads start at about 0.4 GB.
- **Works offline.** After the first visit, the app and any downloaded model work without internet. Install it as an app on desktop or phone.
- **Personas** for everyday chat, precise answers, writing, coding and brainstorming, or your own instructions.
- **Ask about your documents.** Attach a PDF, text or Markdown file to a chat.
- **55 open models,** from tiny to 9B parameters, including Qwen3.5, Llama 3, Phi-4-mini, Gemma, Mistral and OLMo 2.
- **Honest about its limits.** It shows how fast a model writes on your device, warns that small models get facts wrong, and explains problems in plain language.
- **Built to survive real devices.** It detects GPUs that garble 16-bit math and switches to models that work. It recovers when a phone takes the GPU away, and never gets stuck reloading a model that ran out of memory. It also warns before a download that won't fit.

## Known limitations

- **Works best on a computer.** Phones give the AI little memory; on many, only the smallest models fit, and their answers are very limited.
- **Small models get facts wrong,** even when they sound sure. Check anything important.
- **Needs a browser with WebGPU.** Tested on Chrome and Edge (Windows) and Chrome (Android). **Safari (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and Firefox are not tested yet.**
- **Private (incognito) windows** allow too little storage for most models.
- Chats stay in the browser where you made them; use Export / Import to move them.

## Found a problem?

Open **Settings → This device → Report a problem**. It opens a GitHub issue with your browser, GPU and model filled in. Nothing is sent until you submit it, and please don't include private chats.
