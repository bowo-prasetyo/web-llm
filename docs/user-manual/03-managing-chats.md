# 3. Managing your chats

[← Chatting](02-chatting.md) · [Manual home](README.md) · [Next: Personas →](04-personas.md)

Every conversation is saved automatically as a separate *chat*. This chapter shows how to start, find, reopen and delete chats.

## The chat list

The chat list shows all your saved chats. Each entry shows the chat's title (created automatically from your first message), a short preview, the model used, the persona icon, and when it was last used.

**Wide screens (computers and tablets in landscape, 1024px wide or more):** the list sits on the left, next to the conversation, and the conversation fills the rest of the window, with the messages in a comfortable reading column.

* To hide it, tap **☰** in the header or **×** at the top of the list. Tap **☰** again to show it.
* The app remembers whether you like it shown or hidden.

**Narrow screens (phones, small windows):** the list slides in over the conversation.

* Tap **☰** (Chat history) to open it.
* Close it with **×**, by tapping beside it, or by pressing **Escape**.

## Start a new chat

Tap **+** (New chat) in the header.

Your current chat is saved first, and a fresh welcome screen appears where you can pick a persona and start typing.

> **Tip:** Start a new chat when you change topic. The assistant gives better answers when it isn't distracted by an unrelated earlier conversation, and your chat list stays easier to search.

## Reopen a chat

Open the chat list and tap the chat. Its messages, persona and attached documents all come back, and the assistant remembers the conversation so you can carry on where you left off.

## Search your chats

1. Open the chat list.
2. Type in the **Search chats** box at the top.

The search looks through **everything**: chat titles, your questions, and the assistant's answers, not just the previews.

* Results show highlighted snippets, so you can see why each chat matched.
* The best matches come first.
* Tap a result to open that chat.
* Tap **×** in the search box to clear the search.

## Delete a chat

There are three ways. Each one **asks you to confirm** first, so nothing is deleted by accident.

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Chat list | Point at (or, on touch screens, look for) the 🗑 trash icon on a chat, and tap it. |
| Toolbar | Tap **Clear** to delete the chat that's open. |
| Settings | **Settings → Current chat → Clear Conversation** deletes the chat that's open. |

A dialog titled **Delete this chat?** appears. Tap **Delete chat** to confirm or **Cancel** to keep it.

> **Important:** Deleting a chat removes its messages *and* any documents attached to it from this browser. **This can't be undone.** To keep a copy first, use **Settings → Export Chats** (see [Back up and move your data](08-offline-and-backup.md#back-up-and-move-your-data)).

## Automatic saving and recovery

You never need to press "Save".

* Every message you send and every reply is saved the moment it appears.
* If you close the tab, reload the page, or your phone's screen turns off, your chat is still there when you come back.
* If you closed the tab **while the assistant was still answering**, the app automatically asks your question again the next time you open it.

## How many chats can I keep?

As many as your browser's storage allows. The app never deletes chats on its own.

> **Note:** In some private or incognito browsing modes, the app can only use limited storage: up to 50 chats of 40 messages each, and they may disappear when you close the private window. **Settings → This device → Chats saved in** shows which storage is in use.
